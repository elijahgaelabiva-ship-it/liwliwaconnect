Getting to Liwliwa

Liwliwa is in Barangay Sto. Niño, San Felipe, Zambales — about 166 km from Metro Manila. Choose how you're traveling below.

~3.5 hrs by car ~4–5 hrs by bus

Manila → Liwliwa by bus

1. Head to a Victory Liner terminal

Cubao, Pasay, or Caloocan terminals all work. Ride a bus bound for Iba or Sta. Cruz, Zambales — both routes pass San Felipe.

2. Tell the conductor your stop

Say you're getting off at "San Felipe Public Market". If booking at the counter, mention San Felipe so you're not charged the full Iba/Sta. Cruz fare.

3. Ride time & fare

Around 3.5–5 hours depending on traffic. Aircon bus fare to San Felipe is roughly ₱300–₱480. Fares and schedules change, so double-check on the Victory Liner site or app before you leave.

4. Last leg: tricycle

You'll be dropped off at San Felipe Public Market. Tricycles wait right there — just tell the driver your resort's name. Fare to Liwliwa beach is around ₱40–₱100 per person.

Check Victory Liner schedules & fares

Alternative: if Iba/Sta. Cruz buses are sold out, take any bus to Olongapo City first, then transfer to a mini-bus or jeepney heading to San Felipe.

Coming from the north?

Baguio, La Union, Pangasinan, Ilocos

Option A — Direct Baguio–Olongapo bus

Victory Liner runs direct trips between Baguio and Olongapo throughout the day — this skips Manila entirely. Check current schedules on their site, since frequency is lower than the Manila routes.

Option B — Via Dau / San Fernando, Pampanga

From La Union, Pangasinan, or Ilocos, take any bus south along MacArthur Highway and get off at Dau or San Fernando (Pampanga) terminal. From there, transfer to a bus bound for Olongapo.

Transfer to San Felipe

Whichever option you take, once you're at Olongapo terminal, transfer to a bus or mini-bus bound for Iba or Sta. Cruz, Zambales, and get off at San Felipe Public Market — same last-mile tricycle ride from there as above.

Manila → Liwliwa by car

1. NLEX → SCTEX

From Metro Manila, take NLEX, then continue onto SCTEX heading toward Subic (SBMA).

2. Through Subic to San Felipe

Exit toward Subic Bay Freeport Zone, then continue out through Subic town toward Zambales, following the coastal road to San Felipe.

3. Watch for the "Welcome to San Felipe" arch

After the arch, keep driving straight for about 5–8 minutes to the turn toward Liwliwa (look for signage near Bobulon Elementary School).

4. Eco Tourism & Coastal Preservation Fee

As you enter Liwliwa you'll pass an LGU outpost that collects a small eco-tourism and coastal preservation fee — keep some cash on hand.

Open in Google Maps / Waze

Drive time is around 3–3.5 hours without heavy traffic. Search "San Felipe, Zambales" or your resort's name directly in Waze/Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions.

Coming from the north?

Baguio, La Union, Pangasinan, Ilocos

1. Head south on MacArthur Highway or TPLEX

From Baguio, take Kennon/Marcos Highway down to Rosario, La Union, then TPLEX south toward Tarlac. From La Union, Pangasinan, or Ilocos, MacArthur Highway or TPLEX both work — either way you're heading toward Tarlac/Concepcion.

2. Join SCTEX southbound

Get on SCTEX at Tarlac or Concepcion, heading toward Subic (SBMA) — this is the same expressway used in the Manila route, just entered from the opposite direction, so you skip NLEX and Manila traffic entirely.

3. Same final stretch as from Manila

Once you exit SCTEX toward Subic, follow the same directions as above: through Subic town, watch for the "Welcome to San Felipe" arch, then turn toward Liwliwa near Bobulon Elementary School.
