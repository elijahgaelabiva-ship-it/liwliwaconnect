Be a good guest

Beach Etiquette

Liwliwa's coastline stays beautiful because locals and visitors both take care of it. A few reminders before you head to the sand.

Protect the coast

Pack out your trash

Bins are limited along the shore. Bring a small bag for your own wrappers, bottles, and cigarette butts, and use the trash points near your resort.

Leave corals, shells, and sand where they are

Taking shells, coral pieces, or sand as souvenirs disrupts the beach ecosystem and is discouraged under local coastal protection rules — photos only.

Choose reef-safe sunscreen when you can

Regular sunscreen washes off in the water. Mineral or "reef-safe" sunscreen is gentler on the marine life just offshore.

Ask before lighting a bonfire

Many resorts allow beach bonfires but want them contained and away from dry grass or tents — check with your host first, and always put fires out fully.

Respect the community

Keep noise down late at night

Liwliwa is a residential barangay as well as a tourist spot — music and gatherings are welcome earlier, but keep it considerate after quiet hours.

Swimwear is for the beach, not the town

Throw on a cover-up or shirt when walking through the barangay, visiting the market, or stopping at eateries away from the shore.

Follow posted flags and swim advisories

Currents can get strong, especially during surf season and bad weather. Swim near lifeguarded or resort-monitored areas, and don't swim alone after dark.

Eco-tourism fee

Entering Liwliwa, you'll pass an LGU outpost collecting a small coastal preservation fee — it goes toward keeping the beach clean and protected. Keep some cash ready.
